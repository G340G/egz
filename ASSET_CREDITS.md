# EPISTASSI — archival image credits

The game uses archival photographs as photographic face fragments, human cut-outs and evidence-card imagery. Images are loaded directly from Wikimedia Commons at runtime; they are not redistributed inside this repository. Their availability therefore depends on network access and the source host. If an image cannot load, the procedural 3D character remains as a fallback.

## Design references

The harpsichord follows published dimensions of Flemish single-manual instruments after Ruckers (about 1.8–2.0 m long, 0.73–0.85 m wide, case about 24–27 cm deep, roughly 92 cm high on its stand). The lid motto *MUSICA COMES LAETITIAE MEDICINA DOLORUM* is the one on Andreas Ruckers’ 1640 harpsichord in the Yale Collection of Musical Instruments. The cemetery plaque *HODIE MIHI, CRAS TIBI* is a traditional inscription on Italian cemetery gates.
